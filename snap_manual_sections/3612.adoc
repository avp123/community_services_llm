== Requirements

Allow a 20% earned income deduction for assistance units (AUs) with earned income.

== Basic Considerations

Apply the 20% earned income deduction to the AU's total countable gross earned income.
This deduction is allowed to cover withholdings and work expenses.

Failure to report earned income timely will result in the loss of the 20% earned income deduction.
